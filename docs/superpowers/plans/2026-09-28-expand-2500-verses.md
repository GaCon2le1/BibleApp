### Curation rules (learned in the last two expansions)

A verse is **rejected** if any of these hold:

1. Its id is already selected (`lookup` flag `selected`).
2. KJV or BSB ends with `,` or a dash, or BSB starts lowercase (`lookup` flag `fragment`). The exception is a sentence that still reads complete on a card, for example `PHP.1.3` "I thank my God every time I remember you." KJV colons and semicolons are normal and fine.
3. It needs a specific antecedent to make sense ("He", "these things", "this man" with no referent inside the verse).
4. It is one of these near-repeats of a selected verse:
   - a synoptic parallel (the same saying in Matthew, Mark or Luke);
   - a psalm refrain repeated elsewhere in the same psalm;
   - a psalm line duplicated in 1 Chronicles 16 or 2 Samuel 22;
   - an OT line that a selected NT verse quotes (e.g. `PSA.118.6` vs `HEB.13.6`).
5. Its KJV text scores ≥ 0.5 Jaccard against any selected verse in the projection report, and a human reading confirms it would feel like a repeated card.
6. It is a genealogy, measurement, list fragment, or a verse whose second half is a curse or rebuke that ruins it standalone.
7. Its CPDV counterpart is the same CPDV verse already mapped to another selected id (CPDV merged two KJV verses). Drop the newer pick.

Tier guide:
- **Tier 1:** a line most newcomers would recognise out of context. Expect ≤ 10 new; the famous verses are almost all taken.
- **Tier 2:** a strong standalone devotional line.
- **Tier 3:** good, but needs its context sentence to land.

The last batch landed at tiers 4 / 294 / 202, averaging 1.67 topics per verse.

- [ ] **Step 2: Resolve every REVIEW line by reading**

For each `REVIEW` id, compare the BSB/KJV text (`lookup`) with the CPDV candidate and best. Choose the CPDV verse that carries the same content. Known traps from earlier expansions:
- **Shifted numbering:** CPDV shifts +1 in Jonah 2, Haggai 2 and Hosea 6. Song 8:6 is CPDV 8:7, and Song 8:7 is CPDV 8:8. Joshua 21 has 2 fewer verses. The vendored CPDV follows KJV chapter breaks in Malachi 4, Joel 2–3 and Nahum 1 (checked: `MAL.4.6`, `JOL.2.28`, `NAM.1.15` all match at the same number). Do not "correct" them to the Vulgate numbering.
- **Low Psalms scores:** in Psalms the resolver's candidate is right most of the time. A low score usually means Latin-flavoured wording ("hoped" for "trusted", "mercy" for "loving devotion"), not a wrong verse.
- **Merged verses:** if `best` points at a verse already mapped to another selected id, CPDV has merged two verses. Apply curation rule 7: drop the pick in `picks.tsv` and replace it with a new screened pick.
- **Misleading bests:** `best` is not always right (earlier, `PSA.25.18` and `PSA.38.15` had misleading bests, and so did `PSA.130.6`, `ACT.28.2` and `PSA.65.7`). Always read.
